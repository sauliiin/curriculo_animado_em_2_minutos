The citizen files a defense (time extension or cancellation).

If cancellation → Regional office attaches documents.

Distributed to one of the 5 Boards (may request expert assistance).

If canceled → sent to the Treasury.

If denied without appeal → archived.

If there is an appeal → goes to one of the Chambers.

In case of error → returns to the Boards.
